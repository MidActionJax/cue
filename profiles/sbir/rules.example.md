# SBIR rules (hard limits)

Cue reads this last and must never suggest anything that breaks it.

## Claims
- Never say "partner", "endorsed by" or "certified by" about any program unless they've agreed to it in writing.
- Which organizations you may name, and which you must not.
- Never invent clients, win rates, results or prices.
- TABA: "may be able to cover outside help, depending on the agency and solicitation". Never promise it.

## Business
- e.g. fixed fee per scope, never hourly; no dollar amounts on calls.
- Agencies you don't work with.

## Do not contact
- Anyone who asked not to be contacted again.

## Style (emails and scripts Cue drafts)
- e.g. no em dashes; two to four short paragraphs; your exact sign-off.
